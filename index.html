Our story

Dani started baking out of a 600-square-foot apartment kitchen.

What began as a Sunday-night habit — eight cookies, four neighbors, one stand mixer — became a side hustle, then a Saturday farmer's market table, then this.

We still use the same mixer. Same butter from the same dairy in north Florida. Same hand-portioned scoop. No frozen dough, no warehouse, no preservatives. If you order on Tuesday, it's baked Wednesday morning and on a truck before lunch. That's the whole bit.

Frequently asked

Everything you'd want to know first.

How long do cookies stay fresh?

Best within 5 days at room temp in a sealed bag. They freeze beautifully for up to 3 months — just thaw on the counter for 30 minutes.

Do you ship nationwide?

Yes, FedEx Standard Overnight to every state in the continental US. Free over $40 (any size 12+ box clears that). Hawaii + Alaska on request.

Are any of the cookies vegan or gluten-free?

Not yet — we keep the menu small to keep quality consistent. The sugar cookie and chocolate chip can be made dairy-free on request (use the order notes), but it's not the default.

Can I customize a box for a wedding or office event?

Yes. Boxes of 10+ qualify for event pricing. Give us 2 weeks notice, email hello@danisugarfactory.com with the date and headcount, we'll send back a quote within 24 hours.

What's the gluten-free situation?

Our kitchen is not certified gluten-free — we bake with wheat flour and use a shared stand mixer. If you have a serious allergy, please don't risk it.

Do you have a storefront I can visit?

A pickup window in Riverside, Jacksonville — open Wed through Sun, 10am to 6pm. Exact address shared in your order confirmation. Walk-ins welcome for whatever didn't ship that morning.
